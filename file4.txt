Title: “Spider-Man: The Alley Rescue”

It was just another cloudy afternoon in New York City. Peter Parker was swinging between buildings, hoping for a quiet patrol. “Maybe I’ll actually get to do my laundry tonight,” he muttered, already doubting it.

As he zipped past a rooftop, his Spider-Sense tingled—sharp and urgent.

He stopped mid-swing, flipping onto a nearby ledge. Below, in a narrow alley, two men cornered a kid holding a backpack. One of the thugs reached for the bag. “Hand it over, kid!”

Before the boy could answer, a web shot down from the sky—thwip!—snatching the backpack away.

“Hey! What the—?”

Spider-Man dropped in between them, crouched low. “You know, mugging kids isn’t exactly a great look. Did you guys skip the whole ‘be decent humans’ lesson in school?”

One thug charged. Spidey casually stepped aside, stuck him to the wall with webbing, and turned to the other. “Want to rethink your life choices or join your friend in wall-art purgatory?”

The second guy bolted.

Spider-Man handed the backpack back to the wide-eyed kid. “You okay?”

The boy nodded. “You’re really fast!”

Spidey gave a mock bow. “Speedy and sticky. It’s a weird combo, but it works.”

With a quick salute, he shot a web to the rooftops. “Stay safe, and maybe… walk in groups next time, huh?”

As Spider-Man vanished above, the kid grinned.

Back on patrol, Peter sighed. “Laundry’s definitely not happening.”
